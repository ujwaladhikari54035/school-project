Explore Nepal

Discover the most beautiful destinations in Nepal, from the majestic Himalayas to peaceful lakes and historic places.


Mount Everest

Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world. It is located in the beautiful Himalayan region of Nepal.

Pokhara

Pokhara is famous for its beautiful lakes, mountains, and peaceful natural scenery.

Chitwan National Park

Chitwan National Park is famous for wildlife, forests, and the one-horned rhinoceros.

Lumbini

Lumbini is the birthplace of Gautama Buddha and an important historical destination in Nepal.

Rara Lake

Nepal's largest lake, known for crystal-clear blue water, peaceful forests, and mountain scenery.

Upper Mustang

A unique Himalayan desert landscape with ancient caves, monasteries, and traditional villages.
